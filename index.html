Budget Notebook — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Budget Notebook (إدارة مصروفي) is an offline-first personal finance application for managing income, expenses, budgets, and debts.

Data stored by the app

The app stores the information you enter, such as income, expenses, budget information, categories, debts, settings, and backups, locally on your device using the app's local browser storage.

No account required

Budget Notebook does not require you to create an account or sign in to use the core application.

Financial information

Budget Notebook is a personal record-keeping tool. The financial information you enter is stored locally on your device and is not intended to be transmitted to a Budget Notebook server.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app may use the notification functionality provided by your device to display notifications. Notification permission is controlled by your device operating system.

Backup and export

If you choose to export or share a backup, the exported file is created from the information stored on your device. You control where you save or share that file.

Data deletion

You can use the app's Delete All Data feature to remove the locally stored application data from the device. You can also uninstall the app, subject to the behavior of your device and operating system.

Third-party services

The core application is designed to work offline and does not require a Budget Notebook account or a remote financial-data server.

Children

Budget Notebook is a general personal finance utility and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions about Budget Notebook, please use the contact information provided on the application's official store listing.
